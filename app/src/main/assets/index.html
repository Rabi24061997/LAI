How the number is computed

Beer–Lambert gap fraction with the leaf projection function G = 0.5, which holds at θ ≈ 57.5° for any leaf angle distribution:

LAIeff = −ln(P) · cos θ / G  ≈  −1.075 · ln(P) at θ = 57.5°
  • P is the share of sky pixels in the centre window (about ±10° around the camera axis), classified on the blue channel.
  • Take 8–15 readings per plot at different spots. The summary gives the mean LAI and the Lang–Xiang clumping estimate, ln(mean P) / mean(ln P).
  • Values are effective LAI. True LAI needs a clumping correction, and readings above about LAI 5 saturate.
  • Best light: uniform overcast sky, or just after sunrise or before sunset. Avoid the sun in frame.
